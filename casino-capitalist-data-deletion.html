Casino Capitalist: Data Deletion

Casino Capitalist (also shown in-app as Ca$ino Capitali$t) has no accounts, and ZFX Gaming LLC does not store personal data about you on its servers. Your progress, settings, and demo credits are saved only on your device.

To delete your game data: uninstall Casino Capitalist from your device. This removes all data the game saved.

To reset your advertising identifier: use your device settings (Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads; iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking).

Questions or deletion requests about data held by our advertising partners can be sent to bz@zfxgames.com and we will help route them. See also our Privacy Policy.
