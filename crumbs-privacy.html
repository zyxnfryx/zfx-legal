Crumbs Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026. Crumbs ("the game") is published by ZFX Gaming LLC ("we", "us"), Las Vegas, Nevada, USA.

Who the game is for

Crumbs is a family-friendly microgame collection directed to children and suitable for players of all ages. We treat it as a child-directed app for advertising and store privacy. We do not ask anyone to create an account, and we do not knowingly ask children for personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

What we collect

Your progress, settings, and high score are saved only on your device. ZFX Gaming LLC does not hold personal data about you on its servers.

Advertising partners

The game shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation. We configure LevelPlay in child-directed mode for Crumbs. Our advertising partners are:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy

These partners' software in the game may collect limited data directly from the device for contextual advertising, measuring ads, fraud prevention and security, and app functionality. Their use of that data is governed by their own privacy policies, linked above. Crumbs does not use AppLovin or Meta Audience Network.

Data that may be collected by our ad partners

Depending on platform rules and the child-directed configuration, advertising software in the game may collect and use:

  • Approximate location (not precise GPS)
  • Device or other identifiers allowed for child-directed ads in your region
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics

We do not use this data to build profiles for personalized advertising aimed at children.

Your choices

Advertising ID / tracking. On supported devices you can reset or limit the advertising identifier in system settings (on Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads; on iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking). On iPhone the game may show Apple's tracking prompt where required before ads initialize.

Optional rewarded ads. Some optional rewards (for example an extra life) may ask you to watch a short video. You can skip those and keep playing.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, they are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store payment details.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data stays on your device, uninstalling Crumbs deletes it. We hold no personal data about you on our servers. See also our Crumbs data deletion page.

For requests about data held by our advertising partners, contact them as described in their privacy policies, or email us at the address below and we will help route the request.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
