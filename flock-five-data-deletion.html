Flock Five: Data Deletion

Flock Five has no accounts, and ZFX Gaming LLC does not store personal data about you on its servers. Your progress and settings are saved only on your device.

To delete your game data: uninstall Flock Five from your device. This removes all data the game saved.

To reset your advertising identifier: use your device settings (Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Questions or deletion requests about data held by our advertising partners can be sent to bz@zfxgames.com and we will help route them. See also our Privacy Policy.
