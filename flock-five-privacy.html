Flock Five Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026. Flock Five ("the game") is published by ZFX Gaming LLC ("we", "us"), Nashua, New Hampshire, USA.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your progress, settings, coins, and avatar choices are saved only on your device.

Advertising

The game shows ads from third-party advertising networks, delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and its partner networks. These providers may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device type, and ad interaction data to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Their use of this data is governed by their own privacy policies. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings (on Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads; on iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking). You can remove ads by purchasing the "Remove Ads" option in the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data stays on your device, uninstalling the game deletes it. We hold no personal data about you on our servers.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version at this address with a new effective date.
