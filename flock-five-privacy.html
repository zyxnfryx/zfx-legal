Flock Five Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026. Flock Five ("the game") is published by ZFX Gaming LLC ("we", "us"), Las Vegas, Nevada, USA.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. Your progress, settings, coins, and avatar choices are saved only on your device. ZFX Gaming LLC does not hold any personal data about you.

Advertising partners

The game shows ads delivered through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) mediation. Our advertising partners are:

  • Unity Ads (Unity Technologies): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • ironSource / Unity LevelPlay (Unity Technologies): Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy

These partners' software in the game collects data directly from your device. Their use of this data is governed by their own privacy policies, linked above.

Data collected and shared by our ad partners

The advertising software in the game may collect and share the following:

  • Approximate location
  • Device or other identifiers, such as your device's advertising ID
  • User identifiers assigned by Unity (Unity personal identifiers)
  • Purchase history
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostics

This data is used for advertising (showing and measuring ads), analytics, fraud prevention and security, and app functionality.

Your choices

Consent prompt. If your device's region setting indicates the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, or if the region cannot be determined, the game asks whether you allow personalized ads before any ads are shown, and ads are not personalized unless you allow it. Your choice is saved on your device and passed to our ad partners. In other regions the game does not show this prompt, and ads may be personalized by default.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. The game's Settings screen has a "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" toggle, available to all players. It is off by default. When you turn it on, the game tells our ad partners (Unity Ads and ironSource / Unity LevelPlay) not to sell or share your personal information. The setting is saved on your device and applied each time the game starts and whenever you change it.

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (on Android: Settings, Privacy, Ads; on iPhone: Settings, Privacy and Security, Tracking).

Remove ads. You can remove ads with the one-time "No Ads" purchase in the game.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your payment details.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Because game data stays on your device, uninstalling the game deletes it. We hold no personal data about you on our servers. For more on deletion, see our Flock Five data deletion page.

To request access to or deletion of data collected by our ad partners, contact them as described in their privacy policies: Unity Ads and ironSource / Unity LevelPlay. Resetting your advertising ID also breaks the link between your device and data previously tied to the old ID.
