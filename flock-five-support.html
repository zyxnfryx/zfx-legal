Flock Five Support

Flock Five is published by ZFX Gaming LLC. Email: bz@zfxgames.com

Where is my progress saved?

Progress, settings, coins, and avatar choices are stored only on the device. There is no account.

How do I restore No Ads?

On the home screen, tap the VIP button (the crown on the left). If you do not own it yet, the ribbon says No Ads. On that card, tap Already a VIP? Restore purchase. If you already own VIP, the card says New device? Restore purchase. No Ads is a one-time non-consumable tied to the Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I report a bug?

Email bz@zfxgames.com with the device and what happened.
